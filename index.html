What it does功能

Three things, done properly.三件事，做到位。

01

Search once. Find it everywhere.搜一次，全都找到。

One search runs across every source you've added. The same title from several sources is merged into one result, so you pick the source, not the duplicate.一次搜索覆盖你添加的所有片源。同一部作品在不同片源的结果会合并为一条，你只需选择片源，而不是在重复项里挑。

02

Stopped at episode 10? Resume there.看到第 10 集？接着看。

Continue Watching sits at the top of Discover. Every title remembers the episode and the position, even after you switch sources.“继续观看”就在发现页顶部。每部作品都记得你看到的集数和进度，切换片源也不会丢。

03

Save it now. Watch it later.先收藏，稍后看。

Bookmarks and history live together in your Library, each tagged with the source it came from.收藏与历史都在“我的”里，并标明来自哪个片源。

How it works工作方式

A player for your sources. Not a content library.它是你片源的播放器，不是内容库。

CineChase does not host, provide or curate any video. Like a browser or VLC, it plays media from locations you give it. You add content in one of two ways.CineChase 不托管、不提供、不推荐任何视频。它像浏览器或 VLC 一样，播放你指定位置的媒体。你可以通过以下两种方式添加内容。

Sources视频源

Add a configuration URL that returns JSON describing a source's name, API address and type. CineChase asks that API for categories, lists and details, and parses the playable URLs. Supported sources use JSON-based formats such as Maccms/VOD-style APIs.添加一个返回 JSON 的配置地址，其中描述片源的名称、API 地址和类型。CineChase 向该 API 请求分类、列表和详情，并解析出可播放的地址。支持 Maccms/VOD 等基于 JSON 的接口格式。

Direct links直链

Paste an individual video URL, such as .m3u8 or .mp4. It's stored only on your device and played directly, for links you already have.直接粘贴单个视频地址，例如 .m3u8 或 .mp4。它只保存在你的设备上并直接播放，适合你手上已有的链接。

Content responsibility. All sources and links are provided by you. Access, legality and permissions are the responsibility of the user and the original provider. CineChase has no control over the availability, legality or ownership of third-party content.内容责任。所有片源与链接均由你提供。访问权限、合法性与授权由用户及原始提供方负责。CineChase 无法控制第三方内容的可用性、合法性或归属。

Privacy policy隐私政策

Your data stays on your device.你的数据留在你的设备上。

CineChase does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.CineChase 不收集、不传输、不出售个人数据。没有账户、没有统计分析、没有广告，也没有第三方追踪。

Stored locally, never sent to us仅本地存储，不会发送给我们
